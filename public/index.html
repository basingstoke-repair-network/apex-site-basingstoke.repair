Fix It, Don't Bin It!

Our Repair Cafés are here to help fix your broken things, saving you money, and keeping them out of landfill.

Find a Repair Café Volunteer With Us

How It Works

Come along to one of our Repair Café sessions, bring your broken items, and sit with our friendly volunteers while they try to fix them. It's completely free, though donations are welcome

🚶 Come Along

Find your nearest Repair Café from our list below. Just drop in during our opening hours — no appointment needed

👀 Stay & Watch

We work on a first-come, first-served basis. Sit with our volunteer repairers and see how your item gets fixed.

☕ Enjoy a Cuppa

While you wait, enjoy a cup of tea or coffee, chat with other visitors, and learn something new

💡 How You Can Help Us Help You

  • Clean your item — it's much nicer for our volunteers to work on
  • Bring all the bits — leads, chargers, manuals, broken parts, screws
  • Bring batteries if your item needs them
  • Arrive early — we work on a first-come, first-served basis

Note: We do our very best, but repairs are offered on a best-endeavours basis. We can't guarantee everything will be fixable or perfect afterwards, but we'll always try our hardest

✅ What We Can Try to Fix

Our volunteers have recently fixed:

🌱 Garden tools 🍳 Kitchen tools 🧸 Toys 🚲 Bikes 💡 Lights 👕 Clothes 💻 Laptops 🖥️ PCs 🌬️ Vacuum cleaners 🪑 Chairs 💍 Jewellery ✂️ Sharpening 🛋️ Upholstery ...and more

Sharpening and Upholstery are not always available Email us beforehand

❌ What We Can't Fix

Unfortunately, for safety reasons, we cannot repair:

  • Items where we can't test whether they'll be safe to use afterwards
  • Cane Furniture
  • Electric Blankets
  • Petrol Driven items
  • Microwaves
  • Fridges & Freezers
  • Anything our volunteers consider unsafe to work on

Not sure? Email us beforehand with brief details of your item and we'll let you know if we can help

Why Repair Matters

Repairing instead of replacing helps create a more sustainable, connected, and skillful community. Here's why repair matters:

🌍 Reduce Waste

Keep items out of landfills and reduce environmental impact. Every item repaired is one less in the bin

🤝 Build Community

Connect with neighbors and share skills. Repair Cafés are social spaces where people come together

🛠️ Learn Skills

Discover how things work and gain repair confidence. Watch, learn, and try it yourself next time
