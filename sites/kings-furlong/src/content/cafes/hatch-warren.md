Welcome to Hatch Warren & Beggarwood Repair Cafe! We're a community-driven initiative that brings neighbors together to repair items, share skills, and reduce waste.

## Our Mission

We believe in the power of repair to:
- Extend the life of everyday items
- Reduce environmental waste
- Build community connections
- Share valuable skills and knowledge

## What We Repair

Our skilled volunteers can help with:
- Small electrical appliances
- Clothing alterations and mending
- Bicycle maintenance
- Wooden furniture restoration
- Computer and phone troubleshooting
- Household items and tools

## Schedule

Visit us on the first Saturday of every month from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We take a break in December but return fresh in the new year!

## Find Us

We're located at Hatch Warren Community Centre on Western Way, RG22 4UQ. The center has accessible facilities and free parking.

## Community Impact

Every item repaired is a small victory for sustainability. Join us in making a positive difference in our local environment while meeting your neighbors and learning new skills.
