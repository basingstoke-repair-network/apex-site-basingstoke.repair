Basingstoke Repair Network is a non-profit community project. We rely on the generous support of local organisations and funders to keep our events free and accessible to everyone. Their contributions help us cover venue costs, purchase tools and supplies, and promote our events.

## Our Impact in Numbers

*   **500kg+** Of waste diverted from landfill
*   **300+** Residents helped with repairs
*   **50+** Active community volunteers

## Become a Sponsor

We offer several tiers of sponsorship for local businesses and organisations who want to support our mission. It's a great way to demonstrate your commitment to the local community and environmental sustainability.

*   **Bronze (£100):** Your logo on our website and a social media thank you.
*   **Silver (£250):** All of the above, plus your banner displayed at our events.
*   **Gold (£500+):** All of the above, plus a featured spot in our newsletter.

To discuss sponsorship opportunities, please get in touch with our team.
