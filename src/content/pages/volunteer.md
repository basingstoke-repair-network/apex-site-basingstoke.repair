Our volunteers are the heart of the Basingstoke Repair Network. By sharing your time and skills, you can help reduce waste, build community, and make a real difference. We're a friendly and welcoming group, and we'd love for you to join us.

## Why Volunteer?

*   **Learn and share skills:** Sharpen your own abilities and pass them on to others.
*   **Help your neighbours:** Get a great feeling from helping someone fix a treasured item.
*   **Protect the environment:** Every repair is one less item in a landfill.
*   **Be part of a community:** Meet like-minded people and make new friends.

## Roles We Need

We need more than just repairers! There are many ways to contribute:

### Repair Expert

Are you skilled in electronics, sewing, carpentry, bicycle maintenance, or general tinkering? We need your expertise!

### Welcome & Registration

Be the friendly face that greets our visitors, helps them sign in, and makes them feel welcome.

### Cafe & Refreshments Host

Help create a warm, sociable atmosphere by serving tea, coffee, and snacks to visitors and volunteers.

### Event Support

Help with setting up, packing down, and making sure the event runs smoothly.

## Ready to Join Us?

If you're interested in volunteering, we'd love to hear from you. You don't need to commit to every event – any time you can give is a huge help.
