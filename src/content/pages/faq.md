title: Frequently Asked Questions
---

### What can I bring?

We repair a wide variety of items including electrical appliances, bicycles, textiles/clothing, furniture, and more. If in doubt, just bring it along!

### How much does it cost?

Repairs are free of charge, but we welcome donations to help cover our costs. Any spare parts needed for a repair will need to be paid for.

### Do I need to book?

Generally, no booking is required. Just turn up during opening hours. However, for very complex items or if you want to confirm a specific repairer's availability, it's good to check beforehand.

### Can I learn how to repair?

Absolutely! The idea of a Repair Café is not just to get things fixed, but also to learn. Our repairers are happy to show you how they fix things and guide you through the process.

### What if my item cannot be fixed?

Sometimes items cannot be repaired, but we'll always try our best! Even if we can't fix it, you'll often learn something useful about the item or get advice on alternatives.
