Welcome to Chineham Repair Cafe, where we bring our community together to repair, learn, and reduce waste. Join us every third Saturday of the month for a friendly environment where skilled volunteers help you fix your broken items.

## What We Do

At Chineham Repair Cafe, we help repair:
- Electrical appliances
- Clothing and textiles
- Bicycles
- Small furniture
- Electronics
- And much more!

## When We Meet

We're open every third Saturday of the month from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM (we skip December for the holidays).

## Location

You'll find us at Chineham Village Hall on Thornhill Way, RG24 8BQ. There's plenty of parking available, and the venue is accessible.

## Get Involved

Whether you need something repaired or want to volunteer your skills, everyone is welcome at Chineham Repair Cafe. No appointment necessary - just drop in during our open hours!
