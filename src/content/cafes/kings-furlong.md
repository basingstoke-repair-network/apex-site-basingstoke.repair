# Kings Furlong Repair Cafe - Coming Soon!

We're excited to announce that Kings Furlong Repair Cafe will be opening later in 2025 to serve the Lychpit and surrounding communities.

## What to Expect

When we open, Kings Furlong Repair Cafe will offer:
- Community-focused repair services
- Skilled volunteer repairers
- A welcoming environment for all
- Educational workshops and demonstrations
- Sustainable living initiatives

## Our Future Location

We'll be based at Kings Furlong Centre in Lychpit (RG24 9TZ), providing a convenient location for local residents.

## Get Involved

Interested in volunteering or staying updated on our launch? We're always looking for:
- Skilled repairers and fixers
- Community organizers
- People passionate about sustainability
- Anyone willing to help spread the word

## Stay Connected

Follow our network's updates to be the first to know when Kings Furlong Repair Cafe opens its doors. Together, we'll build another strong community hub focused on repair, reuse, and sustainability.
